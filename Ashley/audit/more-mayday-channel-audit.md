## TL;DR

1. **vidIQ was credit-blocked for this audit** (free plan, 0 of 150 credits remaining, resets 2026-07-25). All numbers below come from public YouTube data pulled live on 2026-07-12. Separately: the vidIQ-connected channel is the **main Trevor May channel** (`UCXnWH_cIChvXGhLPIJGoiBg`), *not* More Mayday — so owner-level analytics (retention, CTR, traffic sources) for More Mayday are unavailable even with credits until the channel is authorized in vidIQ.
2. **Channel snapshot:** 16.6K subscribers, 3,365,882 lifetime views, 273 videos, created 2025-06-06 (~13 months old). ~636K of those views came from just the last 30 long-form uploads.
3. **Long-form baseline is strong for the sub count:** median 17,310 views/video (≈ **1.04 views per subscriber**), average 21,188. Median like rate 3.05% — healthy engagement. The channel reaches well beyond its subscriber base (browse/suggested is working).
4. **Biggest outlier: "3 MLB trends that I HATE right now" — 94,432 views = 5.46x channel median (5.69 views/sub).** Opinion/rant format with a giant red "BAD" thumbnail. Next tier: "10 MLB Trades that will happen, GUARANTEED" (2.32x), "The Dodgers have SOLVED MLB's injury problem" (2.23x), "MLB's most dominant closer" (2.17x).
5. **Vague titles reliably tank:** "Will he get involved?" (0.28x), "This Fall of Famer had his first Chicken wing" (0.21x), "The best MLB name ever & the streakiest team OF ALL TIME" (0.34x — two topics in one title). The 4 worst performers all fail to name a concrete subject or promise.
6. **Cadence:** ~2.5 long-form/week (Apr: 7, May: 11, Jun: 9, Jul so far: 3) plus **~1.3 Shorts/day**. Very consistent machine.
7. **Shorts are the weak leg:** last 48 Shorts median only ~3,100 views (avg ~4,958); just 8 of 48 crossed 10K. Long-form median is 5.6x the Shorts median despite Shorts being ~2x the volume of uploads.
8. **Timely + opinionated + insider = the winning formula.** Trade/deadline predictions, "I HATE/they can't keep getting away with this" takes, and ex-player insider explainers (Tyler Rogers, injury/CBA explainers) all beat team check-in recaps (0.47–0.83x).

## Title & thumbnail patterns (visual review of 6 thumbnails)

**Template (consistent):** cutout of the host (cap + glasses) lower-left or center reacting, 1–3 player/figure cutouts, huge 1–3 word text overlay in white or red block letters, occasional red arrow. Dark/desaturated backgrounds.

- **Winners** pair a bold word with a clear emotional read: "BAD" in giant red letters + disgusted face (94K), "WILL THEY GO?" over four tradeable stars (40K), "SOLVED" + arrow at an injured Dodger (38.5K), pixel-font "GAME OVER" over a screaming Padres closer (37.5K).
- **Losers** break the template: "Will he get involved?" thumbnail has **no text at all** (Manfred/Trump/player collage — unreadable intent, 4.8K views); the chicken-wing video is low-contrast with "FIRST TIME" over a subject casual viewers can't identify (3.6K).
- Title conventions that recur on winners: numbered lists ("3 MLB trends", "10 MLB Trades"), ALL-CAPS stake word (HATE, GUARANTEED, SOLVED, NEVER), superlatives ("most dominant"), named stars/teams. Losers use pronouns and unnamed referents ("he", "these teams", "This guy" — though "This guy's start is WEIRDER than you think" survived at 1.44x on thumbnail strength).
- Minor hygiene issues visible: typos in titles ("Fall of Famer", "They cant keep getting away", "Why players think there be a lockout").

## Top 5 optimization opportunities (ranked)

1. **Double down on the opinion/rant listicle — it's a 5.5x format.** "3 MLB trends that I HATE right now" (94K, 5.46x) is the single biggest signal in the dataset. Make "N things I HATE / love / would change about MLB right now" a recurring monthly-or-better franchise, and pull the same strong-stance energy into prediction listicles ("10 trades, GUARANTEED" did 2.32x). Target: 1 high-stakes opinion listicle per week during season.
2. **Kill vague titles — worth ~10-15K views per video left on the table.** The 5 worst videos (0.21x–0.52x) all share unnamed subjects. Enforce a title rule: every title names a player/team OR a number OR a superlative, and the thumbnail always carries a 1–3 word text overlay (the one text-free thumbnail was the second-worst video of the whole period). Re-title/re-thumb "Will he get involved?" while it's fresh — at 5.4% like rate its audience loved it; packaging, not content, failed it.
3. **Fix the vidIQ setup before the next audit.** (a) Authorize **More Mayday** in vidIQ (currently only the main Trevor May channel is connected), (b) add competitors in vidIQ (list is empty — e.g. Foolish Baseball, Fuzzy, Baseball Doesn't Exist, Made The Cut), (c) credits renew 2026-07-25 — free tier's 150/mo won't cover a full audit (~15+ calls x 5 credits); consider Boost if Ashley audits monthly. Without this there is no CTR/retention/traffic-source visibility at all.
4. **Rethink or repackage the daily team-check-in Shorts.** 33 of 48 Shorts sit under 5K views while the same effort on star players/insider CBA takes does 10–21K. Shift the daily Shorts mix toward: star-player anomalies (Cal Raleigh 21K), pitching-mechanics breakdowns (Tyler Rogers x4 hits), and labor/insider explainers (lockout 8.3K, CBA expansion 14K). Team check-ins can survive as a single weekly "around the league" long-form (that format medians ~1.0x) rather than 5 sub-3K Shorts.
5. **Lean into the "ex-MLB insider explains" moat and stabilize cadence at 2–3/week.** The pitcher/injury/mechanics explainers (Tyler Rogers 1.55x, pitching injury SOLVED 1.46x, Misiorowski 1.46x, Dodgers injury 2.23x) are the channel's differentiated lane no highlight-compilation channel can copy — and their views-per-sub (>1.4) says suggested traffic rewards it. Long-form output slipped from ~3/wk in May to ~2/wk in July; with trade deadline and playoff race season (peak topical demand, e.g. "10 MLB Trades" at 175 VPH), this is the wrong moment to slow down.

**Bonus hygiene:** run titles through spellcheck ("Fall of Famer", "cant", "think there be") — sloppy text in an insider-authority brand undercuts the credibility that drives the best formats.
