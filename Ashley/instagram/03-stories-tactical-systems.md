# Instagram Stories: Tactical Daily Systems

Stories are the retention engine, not the reach engine. This doc is the execution manual: exact slide counts, sticker mechanics, daily frameworks, DM funnels, Close Friends, and highlights architecture. For the strategy-level split between connected and unconnected reach, format roles, and DM automation platform rules, see the strategy layer: `../../Carl/organic-marketing/03-instagram-organic.md`.

## TL;DR

- **Stories rank per-viewer, not per-story.** Tray position is decided by each follower's history with you: how often they view, tap, like, and reply to *your* stories. There is no "viral story." The game is training a core group of followers to open you daily — which is why consistency beats any single great story.
- **Post 3–7 slides/day, spaced across the day, not dumped at once.** Exit rate is highest on slides 1–3 (Socialinsider 2025: 23.8% on slide 1, falling to ~13% by slide 9). Slide 1 is the gate — make it a hook, never a repost.
- **Sticker hierarchy by value (as of 2026): question > poll > slider > quiz > link.** Question stickers open DM threads (the strongest relationship signal Instagram tracks); polls are the lowest-friction warm-up. Place stickers in the bottom third — thumb zone.
- **A story reply is the highest-ROI interaction on the platform.** It counts as a DM for ranking purposes, opens the 24-hour messaging window, and lifts how prominently ALL your content (stories + feed) ranks for that person. Reply rate target: 3–5% of viewers is good, 10%+ is excellent.
- **Stories do NOT directly boost non-follower reach.** Mosseri has said stories "aren't typically the best way to reach new people." Their reach effect is indirect: relationship signals from story interactions raise your feed/Reels ranking with existing followers, whose engagement then feeds unconnected distribution.
- **Close Friends is the highest-converting surface a creator owns** (completion 70–90% vs 8–15% public; link CTR 4–8% vs ~0.4%). Build it by opt-in only ("Reply IN"), post 2–4×/week, one ask per 4–5 posts.
- **Reply rate is up even as views are down**: Metricool's 2026 study (24.4M posts) shows story impressions −6% YoY but replies +88% YoY. Stories are becoming a conversation surface. Optimize for replies, not views.
- Trevor-specific: the Mayday dashboard "Do this more" widget already tracks daily IG story posting via the `metricool-stories` sync — use it as the compliance layer for whatever daily framework is adopted.

## 1. How the Stories tray actually ranks (as of 2026)

Stories are almost pure **connected reach** — only followers see them (plus profile visitors who tap your avatar). Ranking is per-viewer:

**The three prediction signals (Mosseri, confirmed repeatedly through 2025):**
1. **Likelihood you'll tap into the story** — driven by that viewer's history of opening your stories
2. **Likelihood you'll reply in DMs** — driven by past replies/reactions to you
3. **Likelihood you'll move on to the next story** — tap-aways and exits count against you

Plus a **relationship layer**: DM exchanges, profile visits, story likes, and (Meta's inference of) offline closeness push you toward tray position 1–3 for that person.

**Tactical implications:**
- **Tray position is everything.** Stories in positions 1–5 get opened; positions 10+ mostly don't. You can't buy position — you earn it per-follower through interaction history.
- **Each story reply compounds.** One reply → higher tray rank for that person → they see tomorrow's stories → more chances to reply. This is a flywheel per follower, which is why DM-funnel tactics (section 5) do double duty: they convert AND they rank.
- **Recency matters within the tray.** A fresh story bumps you; this is the mechanical reason to space slides across the day instead of batch-posting (you appear "new" in the tray 3–4 times instead of once).
- **Story likes** (the heart on a story, added 2022) are a private, zero-friction signal — explicitly part of ranking. Asking for them occasionally ("double-tap if you agree") is legitimate; begging every slide is engagement bait.
- **No punishment crossover myth-check**: a bad story day does not tank your Reels. Surfaces rank independently. But a follower who stops watching stories drifts down in your feed ranking too, because the relationship signals are shared.

### Expected reach reality (set expectations before optimizing)

Story reach rate by follower size (Socialinsider, 161K stories, Jan–May 2025):

| Followers | Story reach rate | Feed post reach rate (same accounts) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5K | ~3.3% (up to 9.5–10.4% per-slide for engaged accounts) | 4.0% |
| 5–10K | 1.5% | 3.4% |
| 10–50K | 0.6% | 3.7% |
| 50–100K | 0.3% | 3.0% |
| 100K–1M | **0.25–0.65%** | 2.5% |

For an account like @trevmay65 (100K+ tier), **1–3% of followers seeing a given story is normal, not broken**. Rival IQ's 2024 report found mid-size brands flat at 1.6%. Do not let a low absolute story view count trigger a shadowban panic — diagnose against these baselines. What matters is whether the *same people* come back daily and whether reply rate is climbing.

## 2. Slide counts, sequencing, and pacing

### The numbers (2025–2026 data)

- **Exit rate by slide position** (Socialinsider 2025): slide 1: 23.8% → slide 2: 20.5% → slide 3: 18.5% → slide 4: 15.7% → slide 9: 13.3% → slide 15: 12.5%. **Most exits happen on slides 1–3.** Whoever survives slide 3 usually finishes.
- Rival IQ (125K stories, 539K frames): retention stays **above 75% through 7 frames**, then degrades; only 10% of brand stories run 7+ frames.
- **Tap-forward rate: 56–66% is normal.** Most viewers skip ahead. Images get tapped forward more than video. Design every slide to communicate in ~1.5 seconds even if skipped.
- **Tap-BACK rate is the underrated quality metric** (median 4.6%, top quartile 6.5% — Rival IQ). A tap-back means "wait, I want to re-read that." Dense text slides, stats graphics, and reveals earn tap-backs.
- **Completion rate targets**: 70%+ is strong (Dash Social cross-industry average is ~70%); Socialinsider's engaged-account cohort averages higher. Below 50% = you're posting too many slides or too disconnected.
- **Reach rate climbs with slide depth** (Socialinsider: 6.3% at slide 1 → 20.5% by slide 6 → peak 37.8% at slide 13). Careful interpretation: this is *cumulative daily audience*, not a reward for length — slides posted later in the day accumulate viewers who checked in at different times, and heavy posters skew toward accounts with loyal audiences. The correct takeaway is **"spread slides across the day"**, not "post 13 slides."

### Prescriptions

- **Daily volume: 3–7 slides.** Under 3 = buried in the tray. Over 10 = tap-through fatigue and rising exits (Rival IQ shows retention degrading past 7 frames).
- **Space slides in 2–4 posting moments across the day** (e.g., 8am / 12pm / 6pm / 9pm). Each posting moment re-bumps tray recency. Never batch-dump 8 slides at 11pm.
- **Slide 1 of the day is the most important slide you post.** It's what shows when followers open the app. Rules for slide 1: face or motion, a hook or question, never a feed-post reshare, never a sponsor slide.
- **Video length: 3–7 seconds per slide** for most content; only exceed 15s when the content genuinely carries it (a great BTS moment, a story being told to camera). Max is 60s/slide but tap-forward behavior punishes padding.
- **Narrative connection between slides beats variety.** Disconnected one-off frames ("here's my coffee" / "random meme" / "gym") bleed viewers. A 4-slide arc on ONE thing (setup → middle → payoff → CTA) retains. Think of each day's stories as one mini-episode, not a camera roll.
- **Talking-to-camera slides** are the single strongest retention format for a personality-driven account — they're unskimmable and feel like a DM. 1–2 per day, not 7.

### Proven sequencing frameworks

1. **Value-to-CTA** — slides 1–4 pure value on one topic → slide 5 CTA (link, question sticker, "DM me X").
2. **Problem–Agitate–Solve** — slide 1 problem ("Why does your velo die in the 3rd inning?") → 2–3 agitate with specifics → 4–5 the fix + CTA.
3. **Interactive reveal** — slide 1 poll with a prediction ("Which grip adds more spin?") → 2–3 suspense/context → 4 answer reveal → 5 follow-up sticker. Exploits tap-back and return-viewing.
4. **Open loop across the day** — morning slide teases ("Big Neptune update at 5pm"), payoff posted in the evening block. Trains twice-daily checking.

## 3. Interactive stickers: mechanics and playbook

Every sticker interaction is an engagement event that (a) raises your tray rank with that person and (b) for polls/questions, gives you data and DM openings. Interactive stories generate roughly **2–3× more replies** than static ones (CreatorFlow), and Dunkin' measured **20% lower cost-per-view** on poll-sticker ads vs. non-sticker (Marketing Dive via IMH) — micro-interactions measurably change distribution economics.

**Hierarchy (highest to lowest value, as of 2026):**

| Sticker | Friction | What it earns you | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Question box** | Medium | Free-form replies → DM threads (strongest signal) + content ideas | AMAs, "what should I cover", pain-point mining |
| **Poll (incl. This-or-That)** | Lowest | Mass taps; warms cold viewers into interactors | Daily warm-up, decisions, predictions |
| **Emoji slider** | Low | Sentiment intensity; identifies your hottest fans (100% sliders) | Gauge interest before launching anything |
| **Quiz** | Low-med | Gamified taps; teaches while engaging | Baseball IQ tests, myth-busting |
| **Add Yours** | Medium | Chain participation, mild borrowed reach | Community prompts ("show your setup") |
| **Countdown** | Passive | Opt-in reminders (users get notified) | Podcast drops, Neptune openings, streams |
| **Link** | High | Direct clicks (any account can use; no follower minimum since 2021) | Sparingly — overuse trains ignoring |

**Placement and design rules:**
- **Bottom third of the frame** — natural thumb zone; measurably more taps than centered/top placement.
- One sticker per slide. Two competing stickers split and suppress both.
- Make the poll answers fun, not corporate: "Fastball / Coward's pitch" outperforms "Option A / Option B." Personality in the options is half the tap-rate.
- Never leave a sticker unanswered as the last slide of the day if you can follow up — **posting the poll results the next morning** is a free slide-1 hook and closes the loop (people return to see if they "won").

**Specific plays:**
- **The Slider Heat-Check**: before committing to any new content series or Neptune offer, run an emoji slider ("How interested: 🥎"). Everyone who maxes the slider is a warm lead — you can see the individual responders and DM the top scorers. This is audience research + lead gen in one tap.
- **The Prediction Poll** (perfect for baseball): pre-game/pre-event predictions ("Does this kid hit 90 by August?") → post-result reveal. Sports content has built-in suspense; use stickers to bottle it.
- **Question-box AMA blocks**: run 1–2/week, themed ("Pitching questions only" / "Ask me anything about the facility build"). Themed boxes get more submissions than generic "ask me anything." Answer 4–6 publicly (share the question, answer to camera), answer the rest privately in DMs — the private answers are relationship gold.
- **The Fill-in-the-Blank**: "The hardest part of youth baseball is ____" — completion prompts get responses from people who never answer open questions.
- **46% of users want brands/creators to offer tips or advice in stories** (cited via CreatorFlow) — utility prompts ("What's your biggest struggle with X?") outperform vanity prompts ("Ask me anything about me").

## 4. Daily story frameworks

The content-mix baseline (ShadowPhone's 40-30-20-10, consistent with broader practitioner consensus):
- **40% value** (tips, teaching, insight — the reason to follow)
- **30% personality** (BTS, day-in-life, opinions, family-safe personal)
- **20% interactive** (stickers, AMAs, polls)
- **10% promo** (podcast episodes, Neptune, sponsors, merch)

If promo creeps above ~15% sustained, story exits climb and tray rank erodes — the audience re-classifies you as an ad channel.

### Time-block template (adjust to audience Insights)

| Block | Content | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 7–9 AM | Personality/BTS slide + light poll | First-open tray impression; low-effort tap warms the day |
| 12–2 PM | Value slide(s) — the teach | Lunch-scroll learning window (Metricool's recommended story block) |
| 5–7 PM | The day's "episode" — 2–3 connected slides | Evening peak begins |
| 8–10 PM | Question box / engagement sticker + any CTA | Peak activity; replies flow when people are couch-scrolling |

### Weekly rotation for an athlete-creator (Trevor-calibrated example)

- **Mon** — "Week ahead" slide (what's coming: podcast guest tease, training plans) + countdown sticker if a drop is scheduled
- **Tue** — Teaching day: 4-slide pitching/training breakdown (Value-to-CTA sequence, CTA = "full video on YouTube" link sticker)
- **Wed** — Podcast day: BTS clip from recording, guest tease poll ("What should I ask him?" question box — turns followers into co-producers and pre-sells the episode)
- **Thu** — Neptune build-log: progress photo/video of the facility, slider ("How hyped for opening?"). Document-the-build is the single best long-running story arc available — construction has native before/after suspense
- **Fri** — Prediction poll on weekend MLB games / hot-take slide to camera (reply bait — hot takes are the highest reply-rate format in sports)
- **Sat** — Day-in-the-life / family-adjacent personality content; lowest promo day
- **Sun** — AMA question box (themed) + poll-results and question-answers from the week

**Batching:** value and teaching slides can be batched weekly and scheduled (Metricool schedules stories natively — already in the Mayday stack). Personality/reactive slides must be same-day; that's the point of them. A 50/50 batched/live split keeps consistency without killing spontaneity.

**Minimum viable day** (for travel/slammed days): 1 personality slide + 1 poll. Two slides posted beats zero — a zero-story day resets tray-position momentum with marginal followers. This is exactly what the "Do this more" dashboard widget should enforce.

## 5. Stories-to-DM funnels

The story reply is the top of the highest-converting funnel on Instagram (DM open rates 80–90% vs email ~20% — strategy level: see `../../Carl/organic-marketing/03-instagram-organic.md`). Tactical mechanics:

**The core loop:**
1. Story with a reply trigger (question sticker, poll, or explicit "DM me the word X")
2. Follower replies → 24-hour messaging window opens (Meta platform rule)
3. Value delivered in DM (link, PDF, answer, invite) — automated or manual
4. Human follow-up continues the thread
5. Every exchange raises your rank in their tray AND feed → they see tomorrow's story → loop compounds

**Numbers to know:**
- Reply-rate benchmarks: **3–5% of story viewers = good, 5–10% = great, 10%+ = excellent** (CreatorFlow)
- Speed kills: responding within ~1 minute vs 30 minutes multiplies conversion severalfold — this is the case for automation on the first touch
- Automation caps: ~200 DMs/hour, 24-hour window (Meta rules; ManyChat-class tools enforce)
- Question/poll stickers drive ~47% more direct responses than static slides

**Keyword funnels on stories** (as of 2026, the standard play):
- Slide: "I put together my full long-toss progression as a PDF. **Reply ARM** and I'll send it." → automation DMs the PDF + one qualifying question ("Coach, player, or parent?") → segmented follow-up.
- Works for: podcast episode links, Neptune waitlist, training programs, merch drops.
- Rule: the word must be frictionless (one word, all caps, memorable) and the payoff must arrive instantly.

**Manual reply discipline (non-automated tier):**
- Reply to every story reply, even with an emoji. An ignored reply teaches the follower not to reply again — you're training your own audience either way.
- Voice-note replies to substantive questions are disproportionately powerful for an athlete-creator: a 20-second voice note from a former MLB pitcher is a screenshot-and-share moment that money can't buy.
- Use "reply with story" (sharing a good response back to your story, with permission implied by public stickers) to publicly reward participation — it visibly teaches the audience that replying gets acknowledged.

**Poll-to-DM bridge:** poll voters can't be messaged in bulk, but slider and question respondents are individually visible. After a high-intent slider ("Interested in Neptune assessments?"), manually DM the maxed-out responders: "Saw you maxed the slider — want details when we open books?" High-touch, high-conversion, zero ad spend. Segment poll respondents into launch audiences (the "Art of Medicine" case: poll respondents → retargeting/qualification pool for launch).

## 6. Close Friends (green ring) strategy

As of 2026, Close Friends supports stories, notes, and even reels/posts to the list, and creators use it as a free warm-tier. The numbers justify it: **70–90% completion vs 8–15% on public stories; 4–8% link CTR vs ~0.4% public** (1kreach, 2026). Green-ring content gets opened *because* it signals exclusivity.

**Build rules:**
- **Opt-in only. Never auto-add.** A 500-person self-selected list outperforms a 5,000-person auto-added list on every metric. Auto-adding fills the list with people who didn't ask and mute it.
- Enrollment prompt: story slide — "Starting a Close Friends list for [specific promise: raw Neptune build updates / early podcast clips / real talk on the transition from playing]. **Reply IN** to get added." Repeat the prompt monthly and keep it in a highlight.
- The promise must be *specific*. "Exclusive content" converts nobody; "you'll see the facility numbers I won't post publicly" converts.

**Operating cadence:**
- **2–4 posts/week.** Daily green-ring posting fatigues the list; weekly is too thin to justify membership.
- **One ask per 4–5 posts maximum.** The surface's entire value is trust; each unreciprocated ask spends it.
- Content mix: early access (12–24h before public), genuinely raw BTS, direct asks for input ("which thumbnail?"), member-only offers/codes.
- Answer green-ring replies within 24h — reply responsiveness is the retention mechanism.
- Watch completion rate: if it drops below ~50%, you're over-posting or under-delivering on the promise.

**14-day launch playbook** (condensed from 1kreach):
Days 1–2 define the one-sentence promise → Days 3–4 opt-in prompt in bio + story + pinned → Days 5–10 post every other day (early access, BTS, one ask) → Days 11–13 one offer that's genuinely better than public → Day 14 audit: completion >50% and link taps ≥3× public = the channel works.

**Trevor fit:** Close Friends is the natural home for (a) Neptune early-access/waitlist tier, (b) unfiltered former-player commentary too spicy for public, (c) podcast superfans getting clips first. It's also a testing sandbox — content that crushes with Close Friends is validated for public posting.

## 7. Story Highlights architecture

Highlights are the permanent layer — they're the second thing a profile visitor sees after the bio, and profiles with coherent highlights see meaningfully more profile interaction (Sked Social cites up to ~30% more vs mismatched/default covers).

**Structure (as of 2026 best practice):**
- **5–8 highlights maximum.** Only ~4 show without scrolling on most devices; position 1 gets the most taps.
- Order = a funnel, left to right: **Start Here/About → best content → proof/results → the offer → FAQ/contact.**
- For Trevor, a working architecture: `Start Here` (60-sec who-I-am arc) → `Pitching` (best teaching slides) → `Podcast` (best clips + where to listen) → `Neptune` (build log + opening info) → `Results` (player development wins, testimonials once the facility opens) → `AMA` (best question-box answers).
- **Each highlight should be a curated 5–15 slide arc, not an archive.** Prune quarterly (90-day review); a 60-slide highlight is a graveyard nobody finishes. First slide of each highlight = hook, last slide = CTA (link sticker still works inside highlights — highlights preserve stickers and links).
- **Covers:** design on 1080×1920, keep the icon inside a ~600px centered circle (Instagram crops to a circle and shrinks hard); flat background, one bold icon, high contrast. Names: ≤9 visible characters — "Pitching," "Podcast," "Neptune," not "My Training Content."
- **Highlights convert new followers from Reels.** The Reel brings the stranger; the profile visit is the decision moment; `Start Here` is your pitch. Every Reels-audit should check where the profile funnel dumps people. (Strategy level: see `../../Carl/organic-marketing/03-instagram-organic.md`.)
- Close Friends stories can be saved to a **private** highlight for repurposing to new list joiners — new members get the back-catalog experience.

## 8. How stories affect overall account reach

The honest mechanics, because this gets mythologized:

- **Direct effect on non-follower reach: essentially none.** Stories aren't recommended to strangers; Mosseri: stories "aren't typically the best way to reach new people." A viral-feeling story day does not push your next Reel.
- **Indirect effect: real and compounding.** Story interactions (replies, likes, sticker taps, DMs) are *relationship signals* shared across ranking systems. A follower who interacts with your stories sees your **feed posts and Reels ranked higher** → engages earlier → early engagement velocity is a distribution signal for connected AND (via likes-per-reach and sends) unconnected reach. Stories are how you manufacture a reliable first-hour engagement base for everything else you post.
- **The retention math:** followers who never see your stories are functionally unfollowers. With story reach at 1–3% for large accounts, the strategic goal is not "more story views" in aggregate — it's **maximizing depth with the 1–3% and slowly widening it** through reply loops. That warm core is also your DM/conversion audience and your Close Friends recruiting pool.
- **The trend to plan around (Metricool 2026 study, 24.4M posts / 375K accounts):** story impressions −6–7% YoY, accounts posting stories +28%, **replies +88% YoY**. Views are structurally declining; conversations are exploding. Any story KPI dashboard should weight replies and reply-rate over views. (The Mayday `metricool-stories` sync + "Do this more" widget currently tracks posting compliance — the v2 metric to add is replies per story.)
- **What suppresses story performance specifically:** reposting feed posts as stories (treated as recycled, low-effort; habitual resharing degrades story reach over time — share feed posts to stories max ~1/day and always add commentary/sticker on top), external-app watermarks, all-at-once batch dumps, and long promo runs.

## 9. Instrumentation: what to track weekly

| Metric | Where | Healthy target |
|---|---|---|
| Stories posted/day | Metricool / "Do this more" widget | 3–7, zero-days rare |
| Completion rate | IG Insights (per-day arc) | ≥70% |
| Exit rate on slide 1 | IG Insights navigation | <20% |
| Tap-back rate | Insights / Metricool | ≥4.6% median; 6.5% = top quartile |
| Reply rate (replies ÷ viewers) | Insights + DM count | ≥3%; 10% = elite |
| Sticker tap rate | Per-sticker in Insights | Polls ≥10% of viewers tapping |
| Link CTR | Link sticker insights | ~1% public; 4–8% Close Friends |
| Close Friends completion | Insights (green-ring stories) | ≥50%, ideally 70–90% |
| Same-viewer return rate | Eyeball the viewer list overlap day-to-day | Growing core |

Diagnostic shortcuts: high slide-1 exit = weak daily hooks. High tap-forward + low replies = content is skimmable wallpaper, add stickers and to-camera slides. Views down but replies steady/up = platform trend, not a you-problem. Views down AND replies down = content or cadence problem.

## 10. Common mistakes

1. **Batch-dumping 8+ slides at once.** Kills tray recency, spikes tap-through fatigue, and the algorithm reads mass tap-aways as "not compelling." Space into 2–4 moments.
2. **Slide 1 is a feed-post reshare or a sponsor slide.** The single most valuable slot of the day spent on the lowest-performing content type. Reshared feed posts are down-ranked as recycled content.
3. **Treating low story views as a shadowban.** 1–3% reach at 100K+ followers is the documented norm (Socialinsider/Rival IQ). Diagnose against benchmarks before changing anything.
4. **Stickers with no follow-up.** Running a poll and never posting results, or a question box and answering nothing, teaches the audience that participating is pointless. Close every loop.
5. **Ignoring story replies.** Every ignored reply trains that follower to stop replying — and reply history is the ranking signal. Even an emoji back maintains the loop.
6. **Auto-adding to Close Friends.** Inflates the list with people who mute it; destroys the exclusivity that makes the surface convert.
7. **Promo above ~15% of slides sustained.** The audience reclassifies your stories as ads; exits climb; tray rank decays quietly over weeks.
8. **Disconnected camera-roll days.** Seven unrelated frames retain worse than three connected ones. One narrative arc per day.
9. **Link stickers on every slide.** Trains blindness. Links earn taps when they're the payoff of a sequence, not wallpaper.
10. **Highlight graveyards.** 40-slide unpruned highlights, default gradient covers, "My Stuff" names. Highlights are a landing page; treat them like one, review quarterly.
11. **Optimizing views instead of replies.** The platform's own data says stories are becoming a conversation surface (replies +88% YoY). Chasing view count optimizes the declining metric.
12. **Zero-story travel days with no fallback.** Momentum with marginal followers resets. Keep a batch-scheduled minimum (1 value slide + 1 poll) for dead days.

## 11. Questions Ashley should ask

**Diagnostic (state of play):**
- What's the current stories/day average and how many zero-days last month? (Pull from the "Do this more" widget / Metricool.)
- What's the slide-1 exit rate and day completion rate right now? What's the reply count per day?
- Who are the repeat viewers — is the same core opening daily, and is that core growing?
- What % of recent slides were promo/reshares vs original?

**Systems (build the machine):**
- What's the minimum viable daily framework Trevor will actually sustain on travel/facility-build days?
- Which 2 weekly anchors go in first — the podcast-day question box and the Neptune build-log seem highest-leverage?
- Is there a DM keyword funnel live? What's the first lead magnet (long-toss PDF? Neptune waitlist?) and is automation (ManyChat-class) set up within Meta's rules?
- Does the highlight rail match the current funnel (Start Here → Pitching → Podcast → Neptune)? When was it last pruned?

**Close Friends:**
- Is there a specific, one-sentence promise worth opting into? Neptune early access is the obvious candidate — is the waitlist mechanism ready to receive it?
- Who owns replying to green-ring responses within 24h?

**Measurement:**
- Can the Metricool sync surface replies-per-story and completion, not just posting compliance?
- What's the monthly benchmark review cadence — and are we comparing against 100K–1M tier norms, not vanity expectations?
