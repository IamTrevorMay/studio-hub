# Long-Form YouTube Playbook — More Mayday + Trevor May Baseball

This is the tactical playbook for the two actual channels, grounded in the 2026-07-12 live audits
(../audit/trevor-may-baseball-channel-audit.md, ../audit/more-mayday-channel-audit.md). Strategy-level
"what should the channels be" questions go to Carl; this doc is how each video performs.

**The two channels, one line each:**
- **Trevor May Baseball (TMB)** — 179K subs, 29.3M lifetime views, median recent long-form ~47K.
  Browse/subscriber channel (44.6% of lifetime views). The flagship. Insider access + first-person is the moat.
- **More Mayday (MM)** — 16.6K subs, 3.37M lifetime views, median long-form 17,310. Shorts-heavy
  (49.7% of views), 3x/week news-cycle grind, growing +300% subs YTD. The volume engine.

## TL;DR

1. **Reallocate TMB's calendar: 3 of every 4 long-form slots go to insider/first-person formats.**
   News commentary on TMB averages ~25K (0.4–0.7x median); insider-access and first-person videos
   average ~120K (2–5x). "Behind the scenes of MLB Travel" did 231.8K (5.0x). News reaction belongs
   on MM — that's literally what it's for.
2. **Lock TMB to one fixed weekly slot.** June's 8–12-day gaps line up with a 47% month-over-month
   view drop (1.58M May → 836K Jun) on a channel where 44.6% of views come from the browse/sub feed.
   Irregularity is directly costing impressions.
3. **Enforce the MM title rule: every title names a player, a team, a number, or a superlative — and
   every thumbnail carries a 1–3 word overlay.** All five MM videos ≤0.55x median failed this test;
   every video ≥2x passed it. That's ~10–15K views per video left on the table, weekly.
4. **Make the opinion/rant listicle a weekly MM franchise.** "3 MLB trends that I HATE right now" =
   94,432 views, 5.46x channel median, 5.69 views per subscriber. It's the single loudest signal in
   either dataset. One high-stakes opinion listicle per week, in-season.
5. **Get the Mayday! podcast out of TMB's uploads feed.** Five 68–81-min episodes at 2.1–5.3K views
   and 0–28% retention (0.1x median) are sitting between 100K+ videos, polluting browse signals.
   Podcast playlist + topic cuts, detailed below.
6. **Fix the Shorts→sub funnel or stop pretending it's growth.** Shorts are 31.2% of TMB lifetime
   views and produced 0–8 subs per hit Short vs. 263–368 per long-form outlier. Every Short gets a
   related-video link to a long-form parent, no exceptions.
7. **Close the measurement holes before the next packaging decision cycle:** authorize MM in vidIQ,
   seed the (currently empty) competitor list, run the refresh runbooks after credits reset
   **2026-07-25**, and start the monthly YouTube Studio impressions/CTR CSV upload — CTR is currently
   invisible in the stack, so every title/thumbnail call is unmeasured.
8. **Build the search layer on TMB.** Search is 3.2% of lifetime views on a channel that can own
   "explained by a real MLB pitcher" queries; "Pitcher DECEPTION, explained" held 55.6% retention.
   1–2 keyword-first evergreen explainers per month (queue in ../audit/keyword-search-gaps.md).

## 1. Packaging workflow (per channel)

Packaging comes **before** production on both channels — if the title/thumbnail pair isn't clickable,
the idea isn't greenlit (see ../youtube-longform/07-ideation-outliers-formats.md).

### Shared workflow, every video

1. **Draft 10+ titles at the idea stage.** Pick the packaging, then produce to it
   (see ../youtube-longform/02-titles.md).
2. **Target ≤50 characters.** ViewsKit's 61,838-video study: ≤30-char titles earn ~2x the median
   views of 80–100-char titles. Exception: search-first explainers can run longer to carry the keyword.
3. **One emotion per title** — curiosity, fear/loss, or desire. If you can't name it, rewrite it.
4. **Title and thumbnail never say the same thing.** Thumbnail = emotion/image; title = context/stakes.
5. **Thumbnail: 3 elements max, checked at 120px** (see ../youtube-longform/03-thumbnails.md).
   Shrink every draft to thumbnail-strip size before approving. Text overlay: 1–3 words, 60–80pt
   bold, ≥4.5:1 contrast, bright against dark-mode UI.
6. **Spellcheck the title. Out loud.** Shipped this quarter: "Fall of Famer", "They cant keep getting
   away", "Why players think there be a lockout", "the Ins & Outs" (lowercase t). Sloppy text on an
   insider-authority brand is self-sabotage. Two-person sign-off on every title before publish.
7. **Test & Compare on every TMB upload and every MM upload expected to clear median:** 3 thumbnail
   variants (different *concepts*, not font tweaks), winner decided on watch-time-per-impression.
   Needs desktop Studio + ~1,000–5,000 impressions per variant, up to 2 weeks.

### TMB-specific rules

- **Lead with the credential.** Winning TMB titles are first-person or insider-framed: "I pitched…",
  "Behind the scenes of…", "…according to players", "Ex-MLB Player attempts…". Only ~1/3 of current
  titles carry the credential; the rest could be any baseball channel. Target: every title signals
  either first-person ("I", "my") or insider access ("what players actually…", "Ex-MLB").
- **Curiosity gaps need a baseball payload.** "I have a confession" did 20.8K (0.4x) — gap with no
  subject signal. Calibrate the gap to 6–8/10 and always name the arena.
- **Kill the "??" and random-caps habit.** One ALL-CAPS stake word max per title, consistent casing.

### MM-specific rules

- **The hard rule (non-negotiable after the audit):** every title contains at least one of — a named
  player/team, a number, or a superlative. "Will he get involved?" (0.28x), "Is it too late for these
  teams?" (0.47x), "Is firing the manager mid-season EVER a good idea?" (0.52x) all failed it and all
  tanked. Pronoun titles are banned.
- **Every thumbnail carries text.** The one text-free thumbnail of the period (Manfred/Trump collage)
  was the second-worst video of 30 (4,778 views). The winning template — host cutout + 1–3 player
  cutouts + giant 1–3 word red/white block text ("BAD", "SOLVED", "GAME OVER") — stays.
- **One topic per video.** "The best MLB name ever & the streakiest team OF ALL TIME" crammed two
  topics and did 0.34x. If there are two topics, that's two videos or one "around the league" weekly.
- **Rescue job, this week:** re-title/re-thumb "Will he get involved?" — 5.4% like rate says the
  audience that found it loved it; packaging failed it, not content. Packaging swaps can revive
  videos (see ../youtube-longform/03-thumbnails.md); there's no algorithm reset penalty.

## 2. Intro & retention standards

Full craft doc: ../youtube-longform/04-hooks-intros.md and ../youtube-longform/05-retention-editing-structure.md.

**The 5/15/30 standard on every long-form upload, both channels:**
- 0:00–0:05 — pattern interrupt (best clip, bold claim, or the rant already in motion).
- 0:05–0:15 — the concrete promise; confirm exactly what the thumbnail sold. "Quality CTR" is live in
  2026: a click the first 30 seconds doesn't confirm is now actively demoted.
- 0:15–0:30 — stakes / open loop, then hard-cut into the body.
- **No cold-start branding.** No logo sting, no "welcome back," no "in today's video" — each costs
  4–10 retention points.

**Retention gates (log for every upload at 0:15, 0:30, 1:00):**
- 0:30 mark: 70%+ = strong, 60–70% = average, <60% = the hook failed; autopsy before the next shoot.
- TMB's current 40–55% average-view-percentage on 13–30-min videos is genuinely good (platform
  average APV is ~24%; only 1 in 6 videos clears 50%). The constraint on TMB is packaging + topic
  selection, **not** the content. Don't let anyone re-edit the middle of TMB videos before fixing
  titles and calendar mix.

**Body editing floors:**
- Pattern interrupt every 90–120s on talking-head content; visual resets every 10–20s inside the intro.
- Re-engagement beat at ~25–30% of runtime and a midpoint reset at ~55–65% (the documented ~15%
  drop zone on 10-min+ videos).
- Sponsor reads: ≤45 seconds, placed after the first payoff or in the final third — never at 60–180s.
- Chapters on everything 8+ minutes: first stamp 00:00, 3+ chapters, ≥10s each, keyword-front-loaded.
- End within 10 seconds of the last content point. Verbal "watch this next" + **two** end-screen
  elements (one video + subscribe). TMB end screens currently drive 0.2% of traffic — that's a
  session-chaining lever sitting unused (see ../youtube-longform/06-video-length-session-time.md on
  session contribution as a 2026 ranking signal).

## 3. Upload checklist (run every time, both channels)

Full mechanics: ../youtube-longform/08-publishing-mechanics-metadata.md.

**T-24h (upload private/unlisted — never straight to public):**
- [ ] Upload finished file; let HD processing, copyright, and ad-suitability checks complete.
- [ ] Title: passes the channel's title rule; spellchecked; ≤50 chars unless search-first.
- [ ] Thumbnail + 2 Test & Compare variants loaded (different concepts).
- [ ] Description: primary keyword in the first sentence (first ~150 chars are what ranks), 2–3
      hashtags, chapters block, links after the keyword text.
- [ ] Tags: ≤2 minutes, 5–8 tags, first = exact target phrase. They're functionally dead; move on.
- [ ] Auto-dubbing ON (baseball is huge in Spanish/Japanese markets — found money for TMB).
- [ ] End screen: 20s, one video + subscribe, with the verbal outro cut to match.
- [ ] Pinned comment drafted (question or open loop — first hour comments feed velocity).

**Publish (scheduled):**
- [ ] TMB flagship: **Sunday 7:00 AM PT (10:00 AM ET)** — Buffer's 1.8M-video study has long-form
      peaking Sunday 10am audience-local, and ~48% of the US audience is Eastern; anchor ET,
      back-convert to PT (see ../cross-platform/01-posting-time-master-matrix.md).
- [ ] MM slots: **Tue / Thu / Sat, 8:00 AM PT (11:00 AM ET)** — 2–3h before the midday/evening
      check-in peaks, mornings-after game slates. Never publish into a live national game window.
- [ ] Instant/scheduled publish, not Premiere (Premiere only for season-launch event videos).

**T+1h:** reply to the first 10–20 comments; confirm processing at full res; check the pinned comment took.
**T+24h / T+48h:** log the retention gates (0:15/0:30/1:00) and first-day views vs. the channel's
last-10 median into the analytics sheet. No packaging panic before 48h.

## 4. Length & cadence prescriptions

| Channel | Cadence | Length target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TMB | **1 flagship/week, fixed Sunday slot** (never a 10+ day gap again) | 13–22 min | 15+ min needs a format justification (BTS doc, challenge arc). "MLB Travel" won at 22:16; "I pitched" at 14:24. Both fine — padding is the only sin. |
| TMB search layer | 1–2/month (can be the weekly slot) | 8–15 min | Keyword-first explainers; "Pitcher DECEPTION, explained" held 55.6% — the format works. |
| MM | **3/week (Tue/Thu/Sat)** — hold the May pace; it slipped to ~2/wk in June–July, into the trade-deadline window, which is backwards | 14–20 min | MM's 25-min+ videos underperform (0.34x; the 36-min episode only managed 0.96x). Cap at ~22 min unless it's the weekly "around the league." |
| MM Shorts | Cut from ~1.3/day to **1/day**, re-mixed (see below) | ≤60s | 33 of the last 48 Shorts sat under 5K; volume isn't the problem, topic mix is. |

- Judge long videos on **AVD (absolute minutes)**, short on APV%. Working bands: 15–30 min → 30–45%
  APV healthy (TMB's 40–55% clears this comfortably); 8:00+ unlocks mid-rolls
  (see ../youtube-longform/06-video-length-session-time.md).
- **MM Shorts re-mix:** shift daily Shorts from team check-ins (median stalls <3K) to the three
  proven veins — star-player anomalies (Cal Raleigh 21K), pitching-mechanics breakdowns (Tyler Rogers
  x4 hits), and CBA/labor insider takes (expansion 14K, lockout 8.3K). Team check-ins consolidate into
  one weekly "around the league" long-form (~1.0x median as a format).
- **TMB format quota per 4-week block:** 2 insider/BTS ("Behind the scenes of…", "…according to
  players"), 1 first-person challenge/comeback episode (serialize "I pitched…" with numbered episodes
  and a season arc), 1 evergreen explainer or curiosity essay ("Why lefties are weird" 2.5x,
  "Cheating history" 3.4x). News/predictions on TMB: max 1 per month, only when Trevor has a take
  nobody else can have. Everything else routes to MM.

## 5. Mayday! podcast / live plan

Current state: five 68–81-min "Mayday!" episodes in TMB's uploads feed at 2.1–5.3K views (0.1x
median) and 0–28% retention. Craft doc: ../youtube-longform/10-live-streaming-podcast-optimization.md.

1. **Remove full episodes from the TMB flagship feed.** Designate a **podcast playlist** in Studio —
   this unlocks the separate podcast recommendation pool (YouTube Music background play, podcast
   shelf, TV-app carousels; ~700M podcast watch-hours/month on TV in 2025). Full episodes only in
   that playlist; clips live outside it.
2. **Never RSS-ingest with static show art** — static-image episodes lose ~90–95% of viewers in 90
   seconds and train the algorithm against you. Native video + per-episode thumbnails only.
3. **Judge episodes on absolute watch time.** 25–35% retention on a 70-min episode = 18–25 min
   watched per viewer — that's healthy podcast math. The 0% episodes are the ones to autopsy.
4. **Open every episode with a 30–60s clip montage** of the 1–3 peak moments before any titles.
5. **Live mechanics:** engineer chat density in the first 10 minutes (Q prompt on screen, respond by
   name — msgs/min per concurrent viewer is the discovery signal). Simulcast YouTube + Twitch freely
   (Twitch dropped exclusivity and stopped enforcing the chat-overlay ban); YouTube is the anchor.
   Never delete a live VOD — **trim** the "starting soon" and dead air, repackage title/thumbnail as
   a VOD, or unlist. Keep a local recording on anything long.
6. **Clip yield quota: 10–20 shorts/clips per 60-min episode** (1 per 5–7 min of talk — five-gate
   scoring in ../cross-platform/04-repurposing-pipeline.md). If the team is getting 2–3 per episode,
   selection is broken, not the material.
7. **The best segments become standalone videos.** "The most UNHINGED baseball takes so far" — an
   80.2K (1.7x) video — is exactly the mailbag/takes material inside every episode. One 8–15-min
   topic cut per episode, packaged as a normal MM video, will out-view the full episode 10:1.

## 6. Analytics review ritual

Full diagnostic craft: ../youtube-longform/09-analytics-diagnostics.md.

**Weekly (Monday, 30 min):** for each upload past the 48h mark, run the tree below; log retention
gates + first-week views vs. the channel's last-10 same-format median. Compare same format to same
format — a TMB news video vs. the news-video median, not vs. "MLB Travel."

**Monthly (first Monday, 60 min):**
- Export the YouTube Studio impressions/CTR CSV for both channels and upload to the studio DB
  (the API cannot serve impressions in this stack — CSV is the only CTR source; without it the
  packaging loop is flying blind).
- Recompute rolling medians (baselines as of 2026-07-12: TMB long-form ~47K; MM 17,310, like 3.05%,
  Shorts ~3,100; V/S 1.04 on MM). Flag anything ≥2x for pattern-extraction, ≤0.5x for autopsy.
- Traffic-source mix check: TMB search % (goal: 3.2% → 6% by season end), end-screen % (0.2% → 1%),
  Shorts-feed sub conversion.
- Review the Audience tab new/casual/regular split — casual→regular conversion is the health metric
  packaging can't fake.
- vidIQ pass once credits reset (2026-07-25): outliers scan on the competitor set, keyword refresh
  per the runbooks in ../audit/keyword-search-gaps.md and ../audit/competitor-benchmark.md.

**The diagnostic tree (run in funnel order — ask which metric failed FIRST):**

```
Video underperformed its same-format median at day 7?
│
├─ Impressions low (vs. format median)?
│   ├─ YES → idea/demand problem or feed gap. Was it a news topic past its cycle?
│   │        A cadence-gap week? A format the audience has stopped clicking 3x running?
│   │        → Fix at the calendar/ideation level. Not a packaging swap.
│   └─ NO ↓
├─ CTR low for its traffic mix? (browse 2–5% normal, suggested 5–10%, search 8–15%;
│   CTR *falls* as impressions expand — a dropping CTR on a growing video is success)
│   ├─ YES → packaging problem. Impressions still flowing → Test & Compare (3 concepts).
│   │        Impressions declining too → hard swap title+thumbnail (no reset penalty).
│   └─ NO ↓
├─ Retention broke early? (0:30 gate <60%, or a cliff in the first minute)
│   ├─ YES → promise mismatch: the intro didn't confirm the click inside 30s.
│   │        Fix the 5/15/30 on the NEXT video; note the pattern.
│   └─ NO ↓
├─ Retention broke mid? (≥4% drop in a short window — timestamp autopsy)
│   ├─ YES → content/pacing: dead segment, long sponsor read, topic switch.
│   └─ NO ↓
└─ Everything healthy but no session/subs follow-through?
    → satisfaction/chaining problem: weak end screen, no series link, nothing to
      send the viewer to next. Build the series rail.
```

**Verdict clock:** first 24–48h calibrates, day 7 diagnoses, **day 21–30 is the verdict**. Evergreen
explainers compound for months — don't autopsy a search play at hour 6, and one flop does not damage
the next upload (evaluation is per-video).

## What I'd need to know (facts that would change this advice)

- **Real CTR + impressions per video** (blocked: the API can't serve impressions in this stack;
  needs the CSV upload). If TMB browse CTR is already 5%+, the packaging push matters less and the
  calendar mix matters more.
- **Recent-window traffic-source mix** — the 44.6% browse / 31.2% Shorts / 3.2% search split is a
  lifetime snapshot dated 2026-05-25. If the *recent* mix is suggested-heavy, title strategy shifts
  toward comparison/next-step framing.
- **Audience demographics & geography** (age/gender/geo tables empty in the DB; vidIQ blocked). If
  the audience skews 13–17, the search-layer topics change (youth drills > per-diem money). A big
  LATAM/Japan share raises auto-dub and title-localization priority.
- **Which device the TMB audience watches on.** A heavy TV share supports longer (22–30 min) formats;
  mobile-browse-heavy says cap at ~15.
- **What the Mayday! show is *for*** — audience-building vs. superfan retention vs. sponsor
  inventory. If it's sponsor inventory, the podcast plan optimizes for stable AVD, not clip yield.
- **Production capacity per week** (Trevor hours + editor + freelancers). The TMB quota assumes ~1
  flagship-quality shoot/week is sustainable; if not, MM cadence is the flex point, not TMB's slot.
- **MM exact recent view counts** — public tabs round to "17K"; owner analytics after vidIQ
  authorization will move the medians a few percent.
- **Whether "AWA Expansion" (launch target 2026-10-01) adds a new channel/show** — if it does, it
  needs its own packaging system and this doc's cadence math gets rebalanced before October.
- **The next CBA/lockout news-cycle timing** — labor-insider content is a proven vein on both
  channels (14K/8.3K Shorts); a hot CBA cycle justifies a dedicated TMB explainer ahead of it.
