# Short-Form Playbook for Mayday (TikTok / Shorts / Reels / FB)

The tactical short-form operating system for the actual accounts: **IamTrevorMay** (TikTok),
**@trevmay65** (Instagram), **More Mayday** + **Trevor May Baseball** (YouTube Shorts), and the
Facebook page. Grounded in the live channel audits (`../audit/`, data pulled 2026-07-12) and the
app's own `platform_daily_metrics`. All times **PT** (audience is ~80% ET/CT, so PT times are
back-converted from ET peaks — see ../cross-platform/01-posting-time-master-matrix.md).

Long-form packaging/retention lives in [youtube-longform-playbook.md](youtube-longform-playbook.md).
The full trend-ops system and master weekly calendar live in
[weekly-schedule-trend-ops.md](weekly-schedule-trend-ops.md) — this doc summarizes both only where
short-form execution needs them.

## TL;DR

1. **Your short-form problem is not reach, it's conversion and mix.** TMB Shorts = 31.2% of 29.3M
   lifetime views but the best ones gained **0–8 subs each** (long-form outliers: 263–368). More
   Mayday ships ~1.3 Shorts/day and 33 of the last 48 died under 5K. Volume is fine; the funnel and
   topic mix are broken.
2. **Fix the More Mayday Shorts mix this week:** kill the daily single-team check-ins (sub-3K
   ceiling), reallocate to the three proven lanes — star-player anomalies (Cal Raleigh 21K),
   pitching-mechanics breakdowns (Tyler Rogers ×4 hits), and CBA/insider explainers (14K) — at
   **1/day instead of 1.3/day**.
3. **Related-video link on every Short, both channels, no exceptions.** It's manual, desktop-only,
   and worth a ~10x lift in Shorts→long-form conversion (0.04% → ~0.5% with link + pinned comment +
   verbal CTA). This is the single cheapest fix to the 0-subs problem.
4. **TikTok (194.5K) and IG (285.7K) are big-but-shrinking** (−42 and −553 followers/30d
   respectively, per `platform_daily_metrics`). They get platform-native re-cuts from the clip
   funnel, not simulcasts: TikTok first (fastest feedback), winners re-hooked for Reels/Shorts
   24–48h later.
5. **Facebook is dead air and free money.** 175K views over 90d but **0 over the last 7d** — and
   Creator Fast Track pays **$1K/month guaranteed** for 15 original Reels/30 days to anyone with
   100K+ on IG/TikTok/YT. Trevor qualifies three ways. Restart FB natively via Metricool (never the
   IG share toggle: −72% reach) and apply now.
6. **Stories are a daily system, not a vibe:** 3–7 slides/day spaced morning/midday/evening,
   question sticker ≥1/day, reply-rate target 3–5%. The dashboard "Do this more" widget already
   tracks compliance via `metricool-stories` — use it as the scoreboard.
7. **Run the clip funnel as a factory:** every long-form → 4–8 scored candidates in PostShow the
   day it publishes; publish only 4s and 5s on the five-gate score; clean masters, per-platform
   hooks, never a watermark crossing platforms.
8. **Trade deadline is July 31 — 19 days out.** Deadline week is the best short-form demand spike
   until October. Pre-build shells now; daily deadline Shorts/TikToks Jul 24–31.

## 2. Per-platform prescriptions

### 2a. YouTube Shorts — More Mayday (the volume channel)

- **Cadence: 7/week (1/day), down from ~1.3/day.** The marginal Short is a sub-3K team check-in;
  trade that slot for edit quality on the survivors. 3–5/wk sustained beats bursts
  (../youtube-shorts/04-shorts-cadence-publishing.md); MM can hold 7 because the news cycle feeds it.
- **Mix (per the audit's outlier data):** ~3/wk star-player anomaly ("Something is up with Cal
  Raleigh.." did 21K), ~2/wk pitching/mechanics breakdown (Tyler Rogers DNA — 4 of the top Shorts),
  ~1/wk CBA/labor/insider explainer (14K, 8.3K), ~1/wk trend Adapt or deadline/moment piece. Team
  check-ins move to the weekly "around the league" long-form, not Shorts.
- **Spec:** 20–35s default; ≤60s always (the 65–180s mid-length clips are dead weight — 36 in June
  at 2,857 median; cap at ≤8/mo per the long-form playbook). 1080×1920, captions in the middle
  50–60% of frame, 2 lines max, hook text on frame zero
  (../youtube-shorts/02-shorts-formatting-packaging.md).
- **Publish slot: 12:30pm PT daily** (3:30pm ET — ahead of the 6–11pm ET Shorts evening peak;
  Friday is the strongest day, never skip it).
- **Gate metric: Viewed vs. Swiped Away ≥60%, target 70%.** Check Studio → Shorts feed section
  weekly; anything ≤30% viewed twice in a row kills that format variant
  (../youtube-shorts/01-shorts-algorithm-distribution.md).

### 2b. YouTube Shorts — Trevor May Baseball (the funnel channel)

- **Cadence: 3/week, each one cut from or pointing at a long-form parent.** TMB Shorts already
  perform (189K "Relievers are weirdos", 100K, 98.7K) — they just convert nothing. Every Short gets:
  **Related Video link** (Studio → Content → Short → Related Video — manual, desktop, every time),
  pinned comment with the parent video, and a verbal/text CTA in the last 3s. The "Relievers are
  weirdos" Short had a natural parent in "14 Perks of being an MLB closer" (112K) — that pairing is
  now deliberate, every time (../youtube-shorts/03-shorts-channel-strategy-funnel.md).
- **Keep Shorts at 25–40% of TMB uploads** (3 Shorts against 1 long-form/wk = fine; don't drift
  past ~55% or long-form thins).
- **Lane: evergreen + insider.** Grip series (one pitch per Short, 60s — Shorts search surfaces
  how-to queries now), "what X is really like" clips from the insider long-forms, and first-person
  comeback moments. These compound via search instead of expiring in the feed
  (../audit/keyword-search-gaps.md, ideas #1, #15, #18).
- **Publish slots: Tue / Thu / Sat, 12:30pm PT.** Same-day Shorts + long-form is fine (the 24–48h
  spacing myth is officially debunked) — long-form 11am PT, Short after 12:30pm.
- **Original audio only.** One licensed track diverts ~50% of pool revenue and MLB Content ID
  claims transformative commentary anyway — keep clips short and commentary-dominant
  (../youtube-shorts/05-shorts-monetization-niche.md).

### 2c. TikTok — IamTrevorMay

- **Cadence: 5/week (Mon–Fri), one slot/day, never two posts within 3–4 hours.** Buffer's 11.4M-post
  data: 2–5/wk is the efficiency sweet spot (+17% views/post); more buys lottery tickets, not
  baseline (../tiktok/03-posting-times-cadence.md).
- **Slot: 10:30am–1pm PT weekdays** (1:30–4pm ET — 2–6h ahead of the audience evening peak, which is
  the correct lead time; never post *at* peak). Optional experiment: a 4:30am PT slot 1×/wk to test
  the sports-audience 6–8am ET morning spike.
- **Completion is the gate: ~70% needed to escape the test batch** (up from ~50% in 2024). Default
  length 21–34s for takes, 60–90s for breakdowns (>60s now earns +43% reach when it holds). Engineer
  loops — end frame feeds first frame; 20–30% rewatch is the strongest single signal
  (../tiktok/01-algorithm-distribution.md).
- **SEO every post:** target keyword spoken in the first 3s, on-screen in the first frames, and in
  the caption's first 50 chars. 3–5 hashtags max (hard cap 5 since mid-2025); #fyp is worth zero.
  Run Creator Search Insights weekly for content-gap keywords — free demand data almost nobody in
  baseball uses (../tiktok/05-tiktok-seo-search.md).
- **Mix: ~60% trend/format-riding, ~40% original audio** (talking-to-camera takes, podcast clips,
  mound-visit storytelling). Original audio is the uncopyable layer. Cap pure trend jumps at
  10–20% of output and ≤2h effort each (../tiktok/04-trends-sounds-lifecycle.md,
  ../cross-platform/02-trend-decision-framework.md).
- **Photo carousels 1–2/wk.** Highest engagement-per-view format on TikTok right now (+81% ER vs
  video) and TikTok's own coaching point to MLB players. 5–10 slides, first slide built like a
  thumbnail (../tiktok/02-formatting-production-specs.md, ../tiktok/06-sports-athlete-niche.md).
- **Pursue the MLB×TikTok creator program** (expanded Feb 2026, gives selected creators
  current + archival footage access). A retired MLB player is exactly the profile it wants — this
  converts the biggest copyright risk into an asset (../tiktok/06-sports-athlete-niche.md).
- **Benchmark against the decline, not 2024:** platform views/post are down ~31% YoY. Judge posts on
  completion + shares, not raw views.

### 2d. Instagram — @trevmay65

- **Reels: 4/week (Mon, Wed, Thu, + 1 flex), at least 1 run as a Trial Reel first.** Trials go to
  non-followers only, insights at ~24h, auto-share if it pops within 72h — the built-in A/B lab.
  Judge trials on watch time + follows-per-reach, never raw views
  (../instagram/05-posting-times-trial-reels-grid.md).
- **Slots: Wed 9am PT (noon ET), Tue/Thu 3–4pm PT (6–7pm ET).** Sports IG engagement peaks
  8–10pm ET at ~5x the morning rate — the 3–4pm PT slot feeds that window with lead time
  (../cross-platform/01-posting-time-master-matrix.md).
- **Optimize for sends.** Ranking order as of 2026: watch time → sends → likes, and DM sends are
  weighted heaviest. Sends-per-reach ≥2% is the bar; every growth Reel gets designed around "who
  forwards this, and to whom?" — for this audience: the group-chat take, the "show your kid this
  grip" clip, the settle-an-argument stat (../instagram/01-reels-algorithm-reach.md).
- **Retention gates:** <15s Reels need 65–70% view-through; 30–60s need 40–50%. Hook text on frame
  one, inside the center 1080×1440 band; designed cover frame on every Reel (grid is 3:4 now)
  (../instagram/02-reels-formatting-specs.md).
- **Carousels 1–2/wk with native music** — music makes image carousels Reels-tab eligible, the
  cheapest reach lever on the platform. Design for saves (≥1.5% of reach); slide 1 carries 80% of
  the outcome: "The 3 grip changes that added 4mph to my fastball" beats "Pitching tips"
  (../instagram/04-carousels-static-posts.md).
- **First-hour ritual on every Reel:** post at the slot, share to Story immediately, reply to every
  comment for 30–60 min (replies ≈ +21% engagement and comment quality is now weighted), seed one
  real question in the caption (../instagram/06-trending-audio-engagement-mechanics.md).
- **Topic profile warning:** the algorithm categorizes the account off ~the last 9–12 posts. DJ/
  gaming/lifestyle content on @trevmay65 competes with baseball inside one topic profile — keep
  crossover ≤30% and watch non-follower reach for dilution
  (../instagram/01-reels-algorithm-reach.md, ../cross-platform/05-niche-connection-sports-athlete.md).

### 2e. The daily Stories system (Instagram)

Stories rank per-viewer — there is no viral story; the game is training a core group to open you
daily (../instagram/03-stories-tactical-systems.md). The system:

1. **3–7 slides/day, spaced, not dumped:** morning slide 8–9am PT (day setup / behind-the-scenes),
   midday 12–1pm PT (content push: new Reel/Short/video share with a hook, never a bare repost),
   evening 5–7pm PT (the money window — take/reaction while games are on).
2. **Slide 1 is a hook, never a repost.** Exit rate is highest on slides 1–3 (23.8% on slide 1).
3. **One question sticker per day, bottom third** (thumb zone). Sticker value order: question >
   poll > slider > quiz > link. Question stickers open DM threads — the strongest relationship
   signal IG tracks.
4. **Reply-rate target: 3–5% of viewers** (10%+ is elite). Replies are up +88% YoY platform-wide
   while story views fall — optimize for conversation, not views.
5. **Close Friends: opt-in only ("Reply IN"), 2–4 posts/wk, one ask per 4–5 posts.** 70–90%
   completion and 4–8% link CTR vs ~0.4% public — this is the highest-converting surface owned,
   and the future Neptune/merch/sponsor lane.
6. **Compliance = the "Do this more" dashboard widget** (already syncing via `metricool-stories`,
   refreshing every 30s, 7-day bars). Goal: green checkmark every day. If the widget shows a miss
   by 5pm PT, the evening slot fixes it.

### 2f. Facebook — the restart

- **Step 0 (this week): diagnose the dead surface.** 175K views/90d → 0/7d means either `sync-meta`
  broke or posting stopped. Check the ingestion logs, then the page itself.
- **Apply for Creator Fast Track immediately.** 100K+ followers on IG or TikTok or YT = **$1,000/mo
  guaranteed for 3 months** for 15 original Reels per 30 days across 10+ days — content the clip
  funnel already produces. It also grants instant Content Monetization access and a reach boost
  (../facebook/04-monetization-timing.md).
- **Native posting only, via Metricool.** The IG "Share to Facebook" toggle costs ~72% of reach
  (Agorapulse controlled test). FB-specific copy, clean master upload
  (../facebook/02-pages-crossposting-formats.md).
- **Cadence: 4/week.** Mon 12pm PT: best clip of the weekend (FB engagement holds into the evening);
  Tue + Thu 12–1pm PT: repackaged Reels (60–90s+ cuts — FB is the one surface where longer short-form
  is structurally advantaged: >1-min Reels drive 50%+ of FB watch time); Wed: one **text/status
  take or question post** — highest comment-per-effort format on FB in 2026, zero production cost.
  Never a zero week (measured no-post penalty). Links go in the first comment, never the body
  (link posts run ~0.05% ER).
- **Why bother: this is the 35–65 audience** — baseball parents, coaches, lifelong fans; 88% of
  Gen X and Boomers. It's Neptune Performance's best future organic surface (Groups strategy is a
  Later item — ../facebook/03-groups-communities-sports.md). Budget: ≤2–4 hr/wk, all from the
  repurposing pipeline. FB is syndication-plus, not native-first.

## 3. The clip funnel (long-form → short-form, via PostShow)

The factory that feeds everything above. Yield benchmark: **~1 publishable clip per 5–7 minutes of
talk-heavy source** — a 20-min More Mayday video should produce 4–8 candidates; a 60-min Mayday!
episode 10–20. If the team is getting 2–3, selection is broken, not the source
(../cross-platform/04-repurposing-pipeline.md).

**Weekly flow:**

1. **Same-day pass (publish day):** long-form publishes at 11am PT → clip candidates flagged in
   **PostShow** that afternoon. The `Clip Video` automation already fires a task to David Korn on
   every new More Mayday video — extend the habit to TMB uploads.
2. **Five-gate score per candidate (1–5 each):** self-contained payoff · hook-able first line ·
   no missing context · an emotional/informational peak · a natural (ideally loop-able) end.
   Publish 4s and 5s, kill 1–2s, re-cut 3s.
3. **Tier the edit effort:** Tier 1 (clean master + native captions/keywords, 5–10 min/variant) for
   the standard flow; Tier 2 (re-cut hook, re-timed length, platform text style) for scored winners;
   Tier 3 (native rebuild) only for proven outliers.
4. **Publish order — TikTok first.** TikTok gives the fastest 24–48h read. Winners (top-quartile
   completion or shares vs account baseline) get a Tier 2 re-hook and go to Reels and Shorts on a
   1–2 day stagger — never simulcast; each clip gets its own diagnostic window. FB gets the 60–90s+
   cuts on the Tue/Thu slots.
5. **Per-platform hook re-cut, always:** TikTok = spoken/provocative cold open in ≤1.5s; Reels =
   visually striking first frame; Shorts = searchable question/claim on frame zero; the original
   conversational lead-in is never the hook.
6. **Hygiene, non-negotiable:** clean masters only (a TikTok watermark on a Reel/Short = ~40–60%
   reach cut); 1080×1920 H.264, captions inside the universal safe zone (center ~1080×1350);
   naming convention `{source-episode}-{clip-n}-{platform}` so monthly reporting can trace clips
   to sources (../cross-platform/03-format-spec-sheet.md).
7. **Vertical ≤3 min = a Short, period** (since Oct 2024). Anything meant as long-form exports 16:9.

**Weekly output target from the funnel:** ~7 MM Shorts + 3 TMB Shorts + 5 TikToks + 4 Reels + 3–4 FB
posts ≈ 22 pieces, of which ~15 are Tier-1 cuts of the same ~8–10 source moments. That's one
editor-day plus change at 5–10 min/variant — sustainable.

## 4. Trend ops (summary — full system in weekly-schedule-trend-ops.md)

- **8:15am PT daily, 15 minutes, fixed circuit:** `generate-trends` brief (8am cron) → TikTok
  Creative Center Breakout sounds → IG trending-arrow audio → YouTube Shorts trends. Log candidates;
  decide in batch.
- **Jump/Adapt/Skip gate, 60 seconds per candidate:** niche fit, runway (sound at 1K–10K uses =
  window; >30K = you're the punchline), ship-inside-48h, ≤2h effort, fallout risk. **Skip is the
  default.** Adapt — trend's *structure*, Trevor's baseball payload — is the house move; a pure
  Jump must carry the MLB-credential layer or it's off-brand noise.
- **TikTok leads IG by 3–10 days** — a sound viral on TikTok with <5K IG uses is a free head start
  on Reels (../instagram/06-trending-audio-engagement-mechanics.md).
- **Moment trends beat sound trends in this niche and they're on a schedule:** trade deadline
  Jul 31, playoff rounds in October. Pre-build shells; during deadline week (Jul 24–31) short-form
  goes daily-reactive on every surface — post clip takes within 30 min of news breaking where
  possible (../tiktok/06-sports-athlete-niche.md speed rule).
- **Postmortem every trend piece at 48h and 7d:** non-follower reach, shares/reach, follows/reach vs
  baseline (../cross-platform/02-trend-decision-framework.md).

## 7. What I'd need to know (facts that would change this playbook)

1. **TikTok/IG per-post analytics** — completion rate, sends/reach, non-follower %, follower-quality
   read on IamTrevorMay. No audit exists for these accounts yet; if TikTok completion is already
   ≥60%, the problem is follower staleness, not content, and the fix changes.
2. **Which Facebook surface died** — sync vs posting vs page issue. If posting never stopped and
   reach genuinely fell to zero, the page may be flagged non-recommendable (originality
   enforcement) and needs remediation before the restart.
3. **Editor capacity** — David Korn's actual hours/week and whether PostShow output is
   editor-bound or selection-bound. The 22-pieces/week target assumes ~1 editor-day of Tier-1 cuts.
4. **Advanced-features status on both YT channels** — the related-video link requires it; if TMB or
   MM lacks it, that's a same-day fix before anything else in §2b.
5. **Shorts Viewed-vs-Swiped data from Studio** for both channels — the audit only had public view
   counts; the real mix decisions should run on viewed% once someone hand-pulls it.
6. **Metricool plan limits** — whether it can schedule TikTok carousels, FB Reels, and IG trials;
   any gap forces manual publishing slots into the schedule.
7. **MLB creator-program status / footage rights** — access changes the TikTok and Shorts ceiling
   materially (archival footage + no Content ID anxiety).
8. **Whether the Mayday! show relaunches** (per the long-form playbook) — a live biweekly show adds
   10–20 clips/episode to the funnel and changes the weekly output math.
9. **Who executes daily** — which slots are Trevor-only (stories, takes) vs delegable (clip cuts,
   scheduling). The stories system fails if it depends on anyone but Trevor's phone.
